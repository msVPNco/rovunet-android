The complete pinned tree, adapters and vendor accompany this private delivery as
Rovunet-macOS-0.1.0-dev.6-native-source.zip. No external Go download is needed.

Go 1.27.3, CGO_ENABLED=0 GOOS=darwin GOARCH=arm64 (or amd64):
go build -mod=vendor -tags with_gvisor -buildvcs=false -trimpath -ldflags="-s -w -checklinkname=0" -o rovunet-tun ./cmd/rovunet-tun
For data-plane tests use -tags with_gvisor as well. The scripts-only Installer
is generated from the final hardened native signature; see scripts/package-tun-service.py.
