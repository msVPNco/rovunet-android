Mihomo source: https://github.com/MetaCubeX/mihomo/tree/3f27ef39f8c96bbfbc8b0b9bb10f5c9ef60149d8
Adapter source: macos/native/extra; macos/scripts/build-extra.py uses an overlay
to build it without changing the AWG/TUN adapters in the pinned source tree.
Corresponding Mihomo source and all vendored dependencies accompany this private
delivery in Rovunet-macOS-0.1.0-dev.6-native-source.zip. The Mac adapter and build
script accompany it in Rovunet-macOS-0.1.0-dev.6-source.zip.
Dependency license texts are included under licenses/AmneziaWG/dependencies.
The explicit import dialects follow Android SupplementalCore at
5e66f167c85aecac26008d930a7a24df6f2a31f2. Full mieru:// documents and multiple
Mieru bindings are rejected; the official single-binding mierus:// format is supported.
