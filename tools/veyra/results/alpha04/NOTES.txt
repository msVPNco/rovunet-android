Implemented:
- Collection card menu: Delete collection, name/count confirmation, disconnected-only guard, chain membership protection, reviewed-profile transactional deletion.

- Distinct panel NoizDNS labels and anonymous Soar explanation, optional heading comments, repeated inferred names disambiguated, export errors separated.
- Partial migration to Android resources in English/Persian/Chinese/Russian, notification and tile localization, Russian plurals.
